# Privacy Policy for Andreios ERP

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 2, 2026

### 1. Introduction
Andreios ("the Application") is a high-performance ERP and Stock Management solution developed for independent entrepreneurs and business owners. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Application and explains our data processing practices, emphasizing our "Local-First" architecture and our partnership with third-party advertising providers.

### 2. Data Sovereignty and Local Storage
Unlike traditional cloud-based ERP systems, Andreios is designed to ensure total user data sovereignty.
*   **Business Intelligence Data:** All inventory records, financial transactions, sales reports, and client debt records are stored strictly on the user’s local device storage.
*   **No Server-Side Transmission:** We do not operate external databases that store your business secrets. Your data remains under your physical control at all times.
*   **Data Portability:** Users have full control over their local database files, including the ability to export or delete them permanently.

### 3. Permissions and Technical Necessity
The Application requests specific Android permissions strictly for functional necessity:
*   **Camera (Hardware Access):** Utilized for high-speed barcode scanning and capturing product imagery for inventory documentation.
*   **Storage (Read/Write):** Required for generating PDF invoices, creating local database backups, and managing product media. This includes the logic to purge cached media when items are deleted.
*   **Microphone & Overlay Permissions:** These permissions are inherited from the cross-platform development framework (Buildozer/Kivy). **Andreios does not activate, record, or transmit audio data.**
*   **Display Over Other Apps:** Used to maintain UI integrity for critical system alerts and floating dashboard notifications.

### 4. Third-Party Advertising (Start.io)
To keep Andreios accessible and free of charge, we integrate **Start.io** (StartApp Inc.) for programmatic advertising. 
*   **Technical Data Collection:** Start.io may collect non-personally identifiable information (Non-PII) such as device make/model, IP address, hardware identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), and app usage statistics to deliver relevant ads.
*   **User Autonomy & Consent:** We believe in absolute user freedom. 
    *   Users are presented with the option to accept or decline personalized ad tracking. 
    *   Users may revoke advertising permissions at any time via the device's system settings (Google > Ads > Reset/Opt-out).
    *   Any interaction with an advertisement is strictly voluntary. Users have the right to dismiss, skip, or close any ad unit at their discretion.

### 5. Data Security and Protection
While the user is responsible for device-level security, the Application employs **Pyarmor-grade obfuscation** and advanced logic protection to ensure that the core management software is not tampered with, protecting the integrity of your business calculations.

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Andreios is a business management tool and is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to update this policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or App functionality. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
